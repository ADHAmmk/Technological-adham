FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers to the most common questions before starting your website.

How long does it take to design and build a website? ▼
Timelines depend on project scope: focused landing pages typically take 3–5 business days, while complete business websites and online stores take around 7–14 business days with regular previews.
Will my website work smoothly on mobile phones? ▼
Absolutely! I follow a Mobile-First approach so your website looks crisp and works effortlessly on iPhones, Android devices, tablets, and large desktop monitors.
Do you help with hosting and domain setup? ▼
Yes, I help you set up your custom domain name and deploy on fast, secure cloud hosting (like Vercel) with HTTPS/SSL enabled for 99.9% uptime.
How do payment and project milestones work? ▼
Work starts with an initial deposit upon agreement, and the remaining balance is settled once you review the completed website and are 100% satisfied prior to final handover.
Do you provide post-launch support and updates? ▼
Yes, every project includes a complimentary post-launch support window for minor tweaks and questions, with optional ongoing maintenance whenever you need new features.
